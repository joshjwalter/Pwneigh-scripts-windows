HPCC4 deployment packaging

Deploy one self-contained hpcc4-simple.ps1. Keep the exact identity marker,
parameter block, guarded entry and named tunables. Configure a trusted original
copy for the actual competition. Preserve its bytes for the developer companions.

Tests, watch/reset, .claude, Git history and documentation are development assets;
they are not dependencies on the competition host. No obfuscator or intermediary
deployment script is supplied. A transformed/renamed implementation must be
separately tested with compatible companions; removing coupled interfaces breaks
their compatibility checks.

Protected recovery metadata, backups and persistence reports remain after optional
cleanup. Scheduling exact-file removal does not establish deletion completion.
Do not delete recovery inputs after a failed/partial/unknown operation.

Unit/CI checks are mocked. Live supported Server acceptance remains: effective
firewall/DHCP/RDP access, actual GPO package/LGPO syntax, native ACL restoration,
SCM/Sysmon health and exact-file deferred removal. No automatic deployment/merge.
